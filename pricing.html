Frequently Asked Questions

What happens after the 14-day trial?

Our team contacts you to understand your salon and help you choose a plan. There is no pressure to continue and you are never charged automatically.

Should I pay monthly or annually?

Both include the same features. Annual billing gives you 2 months free — for example, Standard is $749/year instead of $900 at the monthly rate.

How many staff logins are included?

Starter supports 1–2 logins, Standard 3–5, Premium 7+, and Multi-Site is unlimited. Disabling a login frees its spot instantly.

Can I get a custom-designed website?

Yes. Our Custom Website and Premium Full Package give you a unique design on your own domain, built and maintained by our team.

What is the founding customer offer?

The first 50 salons receive 10% off any plan with that price locked for life — even when standard prices change later.

Do I need any technical skills?

No. Add your services, photos and prices, then publish. If you'd like help, our team can walk you through it.

Can customers really book online by themselves?

Yes — they pick a service, a stylist and an open time slot, then receive a confirmation and reminder, with no double-booking.
