Refund Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

At AnjHub, we want you to be completely satisfied with your website template purchase. This Refund Policy explains when and how you can request a refund.

14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We offer a full refund within 14 days of your purchase if you are not satisfied with your template. No questions asked.

1. Eligibility for Refund

You are eligible for a full refund if:

  • You request the refund within 14 days of your purchase date
  • You have not downloaded or used the template on a live website
  • You provide a valid reason for the refund request

2. Non-Refundable Situations

We cannot offer refunds in the following cases:

  • More than 14 days have passed since your purchase
  • You have already used the template on a live website
  • You have violated our Terms of Service
  • You have requested a refund for the same purchase before

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us at metojoy999@gmail.com with:

  • Your order number or purchase email
  • Reason for the refund request
  • Any relevant screenshots or details

4. Refund Processing Time

Once we receive your refund request:

  • We will review it within 2 business days
  • If approved, the refund will be processed within 5-7 business days
  • The refund will be issued to your original payment method

5. Partial Refunds

In some cases, we may offer partial refunds:

  • If you have used the template but encountered technical issues we cannot resolve
  • If you purchased the wrong template and want to exchange it

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about our Refund Policy, please contact us at: metojoy999@gmail.com
